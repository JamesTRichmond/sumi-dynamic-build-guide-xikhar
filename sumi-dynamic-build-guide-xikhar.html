☐ F01 · Write the pitch, one verb, three pillars · you · 15 min–25 min · Not started

The pitch is the scope fence every agent prompt quotes. Without it, a crew optimises for the thread's headline ('full game') instead of your slice.

  1. Create docs/pitch.md in the kit (after F05) or a note now.
  2. Line 1, the sentence: 'A wandering ronin climbs a mountain of ink toward a red sun.' Edit it until it is yours.
  3. Verb: one word the player does most (default: climb).
  4. Pillars (default): every frame is a painting · movement feels like one brush stroke · quiet over busy.
  5. Non-goals (default): not a finished commercial game in a day · no review scores · no sound claimed as finished unless a person listened.
